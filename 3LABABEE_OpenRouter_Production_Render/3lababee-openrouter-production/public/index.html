Product Photo Tips View guidelines

Follow these recommendations for better advertisement results:

  • Upload a clear, high-resolution product image.
  • Use a clean, simple background whenever possible.
  • Make sure the entire product is visible and not cropped.
  • Avoid blurry images, harsh shadows, and reflections.
  • Keep product logos, labels, and important details visible.
  • Upload one product per image for better results.
  • Choose your preferred viewing angle before uploading.
Important: Product Orientation

3LABABEE AI aims to preserve the original viewing angle and direction of your uploaded product.

For example, if you upload a shoe with its heel on the right and its toe pointing left, the generated advertisement will generally maintain that orientation.

Choose the product angle and direction you want to appear in your final advertisement.

Please note: AI-generated results may vary and may alter small text, logos, or packaging details. Always review your final advertisement before publishing.

The QR is composited directly in the browser after AI generation, so its pattern is never regenerated.
